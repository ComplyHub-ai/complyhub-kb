After any technical audit, diagnosis, or fix explanation, always provide a plain English version without waiting for Brian to ask.

**Why:** Brian works across technical and non-technical contexts. He needs to relay findings to Angela and the broader team without translation effort.

**How to apply:** Technical detail goes first for the record. Plain English summary follows immediately after. No jargon, no file paths, no code snippets — just what happened, why, and what was done about it, as if explaining to a non-developer. Do not wait to be asked.
